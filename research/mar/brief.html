Programmable topological substrate research and twin-core design lineage.

Basic Thoughts LLC · Public edition: 11 September 2026 · Source paper: 5 August 2026

This is a newly written public adaptation of the recovered technical paper. Protected content has been omitted from the file itself. It is not a complete reproduction of the private paper.

1. Research intent

The recovered paper presents MAR as a research architecture whose identity includes the order of its state transitions. It describes a twin-core design lineage and examines how a system’s path to a final state affects whether that design has been followed.

The public research question is whether a final-state measurement is sufficient to establish that an intended process occurred. The source study uses a bounded classical control comparison to examine that question.

3. Selected finding

The source comparison reports that the final-state metric alone did not establish preservation of the intended sequence. Transition history and process fidelity were needed to distinguish the compared paths.

The device-related records also report that the tested derivative did not provide end-to-end acceleration. This limitation remains part of the public account.
