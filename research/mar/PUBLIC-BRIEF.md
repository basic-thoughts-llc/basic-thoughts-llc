# MAR — Public Research Brief

Basic Thoughts LLC · Public adaptation · 11 September 2026

## Research intent

The recovered technical paper presents MAR as a programmable topological substrate architecture with a twin-core design lineage. Its identity includes the order of its state transitions. The public research question is whether a final-state measurement is sufficient to establish that the intended process occurred.
